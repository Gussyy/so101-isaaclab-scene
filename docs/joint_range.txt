SO-ARM101 joint limits and the range actually used
==================================================

The limits are NOT set by this repo. They are read from the spawned articulation, which takes
them from Isaac Sim's so101_new_calib.usd, and they match TheRobotStudio's SO-ARM101 URDF.
Isaac Lab enforces them unmodified: soft_joint_pos_limit_factor = 1.0, so the soft limits are
identical to the hard ones.

  joint            lo        hi     span
  shoulder_pan   -1.920    1.920    220 deg
  shoulder_lift  -1.745    1.745    200 deg
  elbow_flex     -1.690    1.690    194 deg
  wrist_flex     -1.658    1.658    190 deg
  wrist_roll     -2.744    2.841    320 deg
  gripper        -0.175    1.745    110 deg

What the trained pick-and-place policy actually uses, measured over 400 steps x 64 envs:

joint             act min  act max |    q min   q max |      lo     hi | used
shoulder_pan        -3.86     3.93 |   -1.733   0.299 |  -1.920  1.920 |   53%
shoulder_lift       -2.58     4.74 |   -1.203  -0.052 |  -1.745  1.745 |   33%
elbow_flex          -4.45     4.05 |    0.412   1.476 |  -1.690  1.690 |   31%
wrist_flex          -6.59     0.68 |   -0.024   0.674 |  -1.658  1.658 |   21%
wrist_roll          -8.82     4.11 |   -2.744   0.400 |  -2.744  2.841 |   56%
gripper            -10.95     3.00 |   -0.001   0.202 |  -0.175  1.745 |   11%
Reading this
------------
* wrist_roll reaches -2.744, exactly its lower limit, so nothing is clamping the arm short of
  the asset's own range.
* Actions are NOT clipped (JointPositionActionCfg clip=None). The policy emits -11 to +5 against
  a scale of 0.5, which means it is saturating the parameterisation rather than being limited by
  it. `scale=0.5` with `use_default_offset=True` centres commands on the crouch pose and gives
  +/-0.5 rad per unit action; the policy compensates by shouting. Raising the scale would put it
  in a better-conditioned range -- and would invalidate this checkpoint, which learned against
  the current one.
* The narrow columns are the task, not the arm. A 5-second episode, one cube 20 cm in front and
  a goal 20 cm to the side does not need 200 degrees of shoulder travel.
* gripper spans 11%: commanded 0.0 (close) / 1.2 (open) by BinaryJointPositionAction, but the
  measured joint never passes 0.202 rad. Worth a look -- it is consistent with the "no measurable
  jaw travel" result in workspace.txt, and it means the jaw pinches nearly closed rather than
  opening and enclosing.

Reproduce: the table above comes from stepping configs/variants/goal_centre.yaml under the
trained checkpoint and recording per-joint min/max of joint_pos against joint_pos_limits.

Driving each joint to its limits directly (scripts/sweep_limits.py)
------------------------------------------------------------------
Joint positions written straight to the simulation, bypassing the action space, so this is what
the arm can do rather than what a policy commands. 10 of 12 extremes are hit within 0.02 rad.
The two that are not:

  elbow_flex     +1.690 commanded -> +1.37 reached   blocked short of it
  shoulder_lift  +1.745 commanded -> +1.96 reached   settles PAST the limit

elbow_flex is blocked by the scene, not by the joint. Re-running with
articulation_props.enabled_self_collisions = False recovers only about a quarter of the shortfall
(+1.41 -> +1.48), so most of the remainder is the arm folding down into the table. In free space
-- measure_workspace.py, no table, gravity off -- the full range is reached.

shoulder_lift overshoots its own limit by ~0.2 rad and stays there, so PhysX is not enforcing
this joint limit hard against the drive. Worth knowing before trusting joint_pos to stay inside
joint_pos_limits.

Both were originally reported as "stopped short", which is wrong for the second one. The script
now reports direction, because a joint that overshoots its limit and one that is blocked by a
table are different problems.

One measurement trap, recorded because it cost a debugging round: writing joint state with
write_joint_position_to_sim alone leaves the implicit PD actuator holding its previous target,
which drags every joint about 0.1 rad back and reads as a limit that is not there. The target has
to be written too.
